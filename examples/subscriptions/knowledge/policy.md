# Support policy

Refunds: an invoice can be refunded within 30 days of its invoice date, up to the invoice amount. Older invoices go to a person.

Usage packs: a one-time pack adds 100 credits to the current billing cycle for $5. At most one pack per conversation.

Plan prices: always get them from the tools. Never quote a plan price from memory.

Hand off to a person when: the account has custom or enterprise pricing, a refund is outside the window, the customer mentions legal action or a safety issue, or the customer asks for a person.
